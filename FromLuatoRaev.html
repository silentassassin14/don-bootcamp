Angela_Shafi, November

I tried coming with the title first today because I knew what I wanted to write. Idk if this will go exactly how I planned, but here it goes…

I’m hurt. More like was hurt.

This month of November, which is supposedly a month of good things, was really a roller coaster of emotions for me. So I tried naming each feeling that I went through, only to realize there aren’t really enough terms for emotions.

So recently, I’ve nicknamed someone a certain something (more like giving them a pseudonym) so that they wouldn’t know when I talk about them. The name was just something I randomly took from some anime characters’ names split. So it didn’t mean much.

But later, when I was in my little corner wondering about synonymous words of something I absolutely love, this name came up. Crazy coincidence, right? My thoughts went kinda out of control then, and I went to a delusional world.

Infatuation, idk if it’s the right word or not. That thing happened, I guess. Let me decode it.

Infatuation

An intense, often foolish, or obsessive passion or admiration for someone or something that is usually short-lived and can cloud clear judgment.

Was it intense?Idk, maybe.
Was it foolish?Damn sure it is.
Obsessive passion?I don’t think so.
Admiration?Yeh, sure.
Did it cloud my judgement?I think so.

So is it infatuation then? Idk.

Limerence

The mental state of being madly in love or intensely infatuated when reciprocation of the feeling is uncertain.

Madly in love?Definitely not, I think.
Intensely infatuated?At some point, I think.
Is reciprocation uncertain?Certain that it’s not gonna.

In the end, limerence is a bit much. So that isn’t it, I think.

Admiration

A positive social emotion felt when observing someone’s competence, talent, skill, or virtuous actions that exceed typical standards.

A positive social emotion?At first. But the way someone’s action (unintentional, maybe) toys with your emotions is not something I’m happy about.
Competence and the others?They have, I believe.
Exceeding typical standards?Maybe it does, maybe it doesn’t.

So what exactly am I feeling?
I don’t know. I really don’t.

My suggestion is to guard your emotions well. The moment you realize somebody has a bit too much influence on them, get away, which is what my plan is.

Till next time, bye.
(Btw, their new pseudonym is in Estonian.)Yours truly, Angela_Shafi
